# EnsDev Product Manifesto v4.2

EnsDev exists to identify and remove the particular friction people experience with existing software models.

## Identify and Serve the Edge Cases

Our audience is all people, with deliberate attention to the people and situations prevailing software models underserve. A small or unfamiliar need still matters.

## Less Friction, More Possibility

Success includes reduced effort, fewer barriers, preserved capability on difficult days, and increased capability where it helps. People should spend more of their attention on their own purposes and less on maintaining software.

## Cognitive Scaffolding

Software can carry the remembering, organizing, and continuity burden. Information, intentions, and context should remain useful when attention or circumstances change.

## More Capable, Not More Managed

Dignity and agency are central to the benefit we seek. The person's own needs and goals give that benefit meaning.

## A Starting Perspective, Not a Universal Person

EnsDev is informed by its founder's AuDHD perspective. That perspective helps reveal overlooked friction and can also leave other experiences unseen. Our purpose includes people whose needs and ways of thinking differ from that starting point.

This is purpose only. Operational commitments and implementation choices live in the documents identified by [Governance](EnsDev_Governance.md#document-stack).
