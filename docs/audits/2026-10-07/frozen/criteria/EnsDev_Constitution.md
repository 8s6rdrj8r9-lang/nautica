# EnsDev Constitution v6.0

The purpose lives in the [Manifesto](EnsDev_Product_Manifesto.md). The commitments below bind EnsDev products and their development. They may be changed only through a governing amendment, not an exception.

## Human First

Humans are beneficiaries, never products. This covers users and people materially affected by a system, including people represented in another person's records. Human agency includes authority over what is delegated and the ability to revise or withdraw that delegation. A system's interpretation of a person's needs remains open to correction by that person.

## User Ownership

Users own their data, workflows, history, user-created settings, customizations, and AI-generated results. They can export them in usable formats and delete them. EnsDev supports user-controlled portability and exchange across applications. Ownership does not override the privacy or access interests of other people represented in that information.

## AI Is Opt-In

AI requires informed consent and remains controllable. Consent distinguishes local AI from cloud AI and identifies the information used. Users can disable AI while retaining existing work and usable core non-AI functions. Scope of delegated actions is explicit and revocable under Human First.

## Knowledge Must Be Externalized

Critical commitments, decisions, and systems must not depend on a single person's memory or an ephemeral conversation. Useful context supports resuming ongoing work. Externalization remains subject to the privacy and retention commitment below; it is not a requirement to record everything a person does.

## Preservation Over Perfection

Systems and people's work should survive and evolve. Preserve data, settings, and useful context across changes and failures, with recovery where feasible. People's work should be able to outlive the particular app through usable export and migration. Deliberate deletion must be honoured rather than silently reversed. Completion and release are governed separately in the Handbook.

## Graceful Degradation

Products remain useful when dependencies fail. Core non-AI functions remain usable, and unavailable services must not silently expand permissions or erase existing work. Doctrine defines the local-first implementation stance; product specifications define the core functions and relevant failure behaviour.

## Privacy and Context Minimization

Collect, infer, retain, use, and disclose personal information only as necessary for an explicit purpose. Context access is purpose-scoped; access to one feature does not authorize access to everything. Useful long-term history is permitted for an explicit purpose, with inspection, correction, export, deletion, and retention controls. Information about non-users is covered. Decision records and recovery history obey these same limits.
