# Audit Risk v0.1

## Purpose

Represent risk without collapsing unlike dimensions into a misleading
score.

## Risk profile

Assess where applicable:

-   **Severity** --- magnitude of credible consequence.
-   **Likelihood** --- probability/frequency under stated conditions.
-   **Exposure** --- who/what encounters the pathway and how often.
-   **Blast Radius** --- scope of affected subjects/systems.
-   **Reversibility** --- ease and completeness of recovery.
-   **Confidence** --- strength of evidence supporting the assessment.
-   **Detectability** --- likelihood the condition is discovered before
    consequence.

Do not average these into a universal project-health number.

## Severity

Use an ordinal S0--S5 model:

-   S0 negligible
-   S1 minor
-   S2 moderate
-   S3 serious
-   S4 severe
-   S5 catastrophic

Specialist modules map domain-specific consequences into this common
scale.

## Unknown likelihood

Unknown likelihood is not low likelihood. Preserve uncertainty and
increase investigation priority where consequence is high.

## Risk band, urgency, priority

These are separate:

-   **Risk band** summarizes consequence/pathway concern.
-   **Urgency** describes how quickly action is needed.
-   **Priority** describes sequencing considering risk, dependencies,
    leverage, and constraints.
-   **Effort** describes remediation cost/complexity.
-   **Leverage** describes how many problems or risks an action may
    address.

Difficulty never lowers inherent risk.

## Residual risk

After remediation, preserve the original risk profile and assess a new
residual profile. Residual risk acceptance belongs to authorized
governance.

## Compound risk

Multiple individually acceptable conditions may interact into
unacceptable risk. Cross-domain and change-interaction analysis must
look for this explicitly.
