# Verification Classes — Quick Guide

This is a working aid. The framework remains controlling if this guide and a normative framework file ever differ.

| Class | Purpose | Typical question |
|---|---|---|
| A | Requirement evaluation | What evidence is necessary to determine whether the requirement was met? |
| B | Finding closure | What verification does the governing closure rule require before this finding may close? |
| C | Conflict resolution | What verification is necessary to resolve a material evidence or authority conflict? |
| D | Optional confidence / corroboration | What additional evidence could increase confidence without becoming a mandatory gate? |

## Guardrails

**Class A:** Do not prescribe a special method, independent verifier, or job title unless the governing criteria, project evidence rules, active profile/target assurance, or material provenance requirements make it necessary.

**Class B:** Corrective work and closure are separate propositions. Evidence that a repair happened does not automatically satisfy an expressly required closure check. Conversely, once the governing closure condition is satisfied, do not invent another closure gate.

**Class C:** A gap is not automatically a conflict. Use Class C where material evidence or authority actually conflicts and the conflict prevents the determination.

**Class D:** Keep optional corroboration optional. Photographs, a second observer, duplicate tests, telemetry, or another record can be useful without being mandatory.

## Historical evidence

A later observation normally establishes a later state. It does not automatically establish an earlier audited state. Retrospective evidence must actually support the historical proposition being determined.
